How it works

  1. Enter before the deadline. Award and Finals picks lock when the 2027 play-in tournament tips off (mid-April 2027). The playoff bracket opens right after and locks before Game 1 of the first round.
  2. Submit in the group chat. This site cannot store entries itself. Fill the form, copy the text, paste it to Miguel. He adds it to the leaderboard.
  3. Scoring is automatic. Once official results are in (awards during the playoffs, the Finals in June), the leaderboard scores itself.
  4. Winner takes the pot. Most points wins. Tiebreak: correct champion, then correct MVP, then earliest submission.
