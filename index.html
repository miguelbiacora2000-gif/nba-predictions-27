How it works

  1. Enter before the deadline. Award picks lock when the 2027 play-in tournament tips off (mid-April 2027). The playoff bracket on its own site opens right after and locks before Game 1 of the first round.
  2. Submit in the group chat. This site cannot store entries itself. Fill the form, copy the text, paste it to Miguel. He adds it to the leaderboard.
  3. Scoring is automatic. Once official results are in (awards are announced during the playoffs, All-NBA teams shortly after), the leaderboard scores itself.
  4. Winner takes the pot. Most points wins. Tiebreak: correct MVP, then correct DPOY, then earliest submission.
